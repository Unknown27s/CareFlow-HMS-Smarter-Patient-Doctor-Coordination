## ❓ Anticipated Questions & Answers

**Q: How is this different from existing solutions?**
> A: CareFlow is open-source, works offline on local networks, and includes AI assistance - most solutions lack real-time WebSocket updates.

**Q: What about data privacy?**
> A: All data stored locally on hospital servers. No cloud dependency. HIPAA-ready architecture.

**Q: Can it scale?**
> A: Yes! SQLite for single clinics, easily upgradeable to PostgreSQL for multi-hospital networks.

**Q: Why these technologies?**
> A: Node.js for speed, WebSocket for real-time, SQLite for simplicity, Gemini AI for smart assistance.
