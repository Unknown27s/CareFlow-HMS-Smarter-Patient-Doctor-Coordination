# 🏥 CareFlow HMS
## Smarter Patient-Doctor Coordination

---

## 🎯 The Problem

**Hospital queues are broken.**

- ⏰ Patients wait **2-4 hours** without knowing their position
- 📋 Paper-based systems cause **lost records** and **delays**
- 😰 No real-time updates = **frustrated patients**
- 🏥 Staff waste time on **manual coordination**

> *"In India alone, patients spend an average of 3 hours waiting at OPDs"* - NITI Aayog Report

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**CareFlow HMS** - A modern, real-time hospital queue management system that:

### ✅ For Patients
- 📱 **Self-registration** via QR code scan
- 🔢 **Real-time queue position** updates
- 🌐 **Bilingual support** (English + Tamil)
- 🤖 **AI Health Assistant** (MediBot) for instant guidance

### ✅ For Doctors
- 📊 **Live dashboard** with patient queue
- 👆 **One-click** patient status updates
- 📈 **Department-wise** patient tracking
- ⚡ **WebSocket** real-time sync

### ✅ For Hospital Admin
- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor management** system
- 📊 **Analytics dashboard**
- 🔐 **Secure authentication**
- 📋 **Complete patient records**

## 🎬 Demo Flow

```
1. Patient scans QR code at hospital entrance
         ↓
2. Fills quick registration form (name, symptoms)
         ↓
3. Receives unique token (e.g., GEN001)
         ↓
4. Tracks real-time position on phone
         ↓
5. Gets notified when it's their turn
         ↓
6. Doctor sees patient info, updates status
         ↓
7. Complete visit recorded in database
```
